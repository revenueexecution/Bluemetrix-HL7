The problem

Standard PHI discovery checks the fields it already knows about. HL7 v2 doesn't stay inside them.

HL7 v2's flexibility — the same trait that let it survive thirty years of hospital IT — is exactly why identifiers end up in places no schema anticipated.

Structured, but inconsistent

PID and PV1 segments carry the obvious identifiers, but optional-field usage varies hospital to hospital and vendor to vendor. What's in field 5 at one site is repurposed at another.

Free text, unbounded

OBX and NTE narrative fields are where clinicians actually write — and where names, relatives, phone numbers and locations end up with no structure to catch them.

Custom Z-segments

Hospital-specific extensions carry local identifiers — SSNs, referring physicians, internal case numbers — with no standard mapping and no vendor tooling that knows to look.

Embedded documents

Base64 attachments — scanned discharge summaries, referral letters, imaging reports — travel inside OBX segments as opaque blobs that field-level discovery never opens.

⚠
The operational cost: DSPM and DLP tooling built for structured databases treats these HL7 feeds as a blind spot — remediation happens at rest, after the fact, if it happens at all. The message has already moved on to every downstream system by then.
Can we discover and protect PHI before it reaches a downstream system that was never built to protect it?
How it works

Ingest → Discover → Tokenise → Use

Four steps, and none of them require re-engineering the interface engine already moving your HL7 traffic.

01

Ingest real-world HL7

SecureToken reads HL7 v2 messages as they move — structured segments, free-text narrative and embedded attachments together, not as separate pipelines.

02

Discover across every path

Deterministic mapping catches known structured fields. An AI detection ensemble reads clinical narrative for names, contacts and context. Content-type inspection opens embedded documents.

03

Tokenise, format-preserving

Every discovered value is replaced with a token that preserves format and referential consistency — the message still validates, still parses, still means the same thing structurally.

04

Use downstream, safely

Protected messages flow on to EHR, analytics and AI systems unmodified in structure. Rehydration is available only under policy control, to authorised systems.
